## Access Control Matrix

| Security Group | Authorized Access | Restrictions |
|---|---|---|
| SG-IT-Users | IT support tools | No automatic admin privileges |
| SG-Finance-Users | Accounting and financial applications | No HR employee records |
| SG-HR-Users | HR and employee management systems | No financial administration |
| SG-Operations-Users | Operations and workflow systems | No privileged IT access |
| SG-IT-Admins | Approved administrative functions | Separate privileged account and approval required |

## Access Control Policies

1. Employees receive access based on their department and approved job responsibilities.
2. Employees changing departments must have unnecessary permissions removed.
3. Administrative privileges require separate approval and must not be granted through standard employee groups.
4. Departing employees must have their accounts disabled and access revoked promptly according to the termination process.
